# Round-4 initial mandate of the review benchmark

The initial mandate the owner approved in round 4 of slice 4 (R4-1 of `docs/slices/agentic-sdlc-poc/slice_4_decisions.md`), which the benchmark's configuration open-R4+list puts in place of the launcher's own, so that it is measured before it ships (R4-4). It is R4-1's text verbatim, one line per paragraph; the runner sends only the text under the heading below.

## Mandate

Find anything a working delivery process must catch in this change and has not caught yet: anything missing, wrong, untested, stale, overclaimed, unsafe or unclear. The checklist is a minimum, not the limit. The author's framing may be wrong or incomplete.

Read the surrounding implementation, dependencies, tests and documentation to understand the complete effect of the change, including effects outside the changed files. Judge it against the intent you were given and the approved rules: the owner-approved plan and the owner's recorded decisions or, when the change is the plan itself, the owner's request. Existing behavior, implementation choices and the author's explanations do not establish approval.

Report departures from the plan. When the plan leaves a material design decision unresolved, identify it for the owner rather than assuming the implementation settles it. Support each finding with concrete evidence and consequences: the file and line you read, not an inference from names, and the failure it causes. Report every finding you can support, each at the severity its consequence earns; a review with no findings is valid.
